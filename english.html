Учителям английского языка

Oksana Konstantinovna
and Oksana Nikolaevna!

Letters, words,
and books we share, You build a bridge
beyond compare. You teach us how
to speak and write, And make the foreign
world feel right. You teach us how to find our voice, And in each culture, to rejoice. To see the beauty in a phrase, And navigate the language maze. May inspiration never fade, For every memory we have made. May joy and passion brightly burn, With every page and every turn. Thank you for the worlds
you show, For the seeds of thought you sow. Thank you for the gift of speech, That makes our wide horizons rich.

With love, students of 5A ❤️
